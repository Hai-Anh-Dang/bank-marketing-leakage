# Bank Marketing data provenance

Moro, S., Rita, P., and Cortez, P. (2014). *Bank Marketing*. UCI Machine Learning Repository. DOI [10.24432/C5K306](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5K306).

Source: [UCI Bank Marketing](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/222/bank+marketing). Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Metadata and licence checked in this session, 7 October 2026 UTC.

This project bundles `bank-additional-full.csv` and its corresponding `bank-additional-names.txt` from the archive's nested `bank-additional.zip`. Both preserve their original bytes. Hashes and the download source are in `provenance.json`. The notebook reads the semicolon-delimited CSV with its quoted category values. Dataset terms are also documented in [dataset licence and attribution](#dataset-licence-and-attribution).

The dataset-specific dictionary documents 41,188 records, 20 inputs plus a binary output, original date order from May 2008 to November 2010, categorical `unknown` labels and the `pdays == 999` no-prior-contact sentinel. The UCI page also covers older file variants with different attributes and sentinel meanings; those variants are not used here.

Related study: Moro, S., Cortez, P., and Rita, P. (2014), *A data-driven approach to predict the success of bank telemarketing*, DOI [10.1016/j.dss.2014.03.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dss.2014.03.001).
